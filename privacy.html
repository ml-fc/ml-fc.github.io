Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 September 2026

This policy explains how the Manor Lakes Football Club application (the “App”) collects, uses, stores and protects information about players and administrators.

Information we collect

  • Account details, including player name, email address, WhatsApp phone number and password hash.
  • A profile photograph supplied by the player.
  • Football participation information, including availability, team selections, positions, match events, ratings, votes, player status and season statistics.
  • Session, device and push-notification subscription information needed to operate login and notifications.
  • Administrative and security records needed to protect accounts and record important administrator actions.

How we use information

We use this information to operate club accounts, manage matches and availability, prepare team sheets, calculate player statistics, display player cards, send club notifications, recover accounts and protect the App from misuse.

Google and Gmail data

The App uses the Gmail API only to send account-recovery and operational emails from the club mailbox. It requests the gmail.send permission. The App does not read, download, modify or delete messages from the club mailbox, and it does not access a player’s Gmail account.

Information received from Google APIs is used and transferred in accordance with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Storage and sharing

Application data is stored using Cloudflare services. The public website is hosted by GitHub Pages. Email delivery uses Google’s Gmail API. We do not sell personal information. Information is shared only with service providers needed to operate the App, with authorised club administrators, when the player directs us to share it, or when required by law.

Public information

Player names, profile photographs and football statistics may appear in team sheets, leaderboards, match summaries and shareable club graphics. Email addresses, phone numbers, password hashes, session tokens and push-subscription details are not intended for public display.

Retention and deletion

We retain account and football records while they are needed for club operations, historical statistics, security and legal obligations. A player may request correction or deletion of their account information by contacting the club. Some match-history records may be retained in de-identified or historical form where reasonably required to preserve team results and statistics.

Security

We use access controls, encrypted connections, hashed passwords, expiring sessions and restricted service credentials. No internet service can guarantee absolute security, so players should use a unique password and promptly report suspected misuse.

Your choices

You may review or update your email address, phone number, photo and password through your profile. You may disable browser notifications in your device settings. To request access, correction or deletion, contact us using the address below.

Contact

Questions and privacy requests can be sent to manorlakes.football@gmail.com.
